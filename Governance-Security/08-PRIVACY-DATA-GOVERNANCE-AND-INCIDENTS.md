# Privacy governance and personal-data protection

## Purpose

Privacy governance ensures personal or sensitive data is collected, processed, shared, retained, and disposed of in a secure and lawful manner.

## Privacy-by-design lifecycle

1. Identify purpose, data categories, and roles
2. Confirm lawful basis or other required authority
3. Minimize data and prevent overcollection
4. Classify access, encryption, retention, and deletion rules
5. Assess threats, transfers, and supplier exposure
6. Build rights-request and incident response workflows
7. Monitor processing and corrective actions
8. Retire systems and data safely

## Security operations and privacy

SOC logs, investigation evidence, support records, and threat intelligence can include personal or sensitive data. Control access, limit exposure, and authorize use only for the stated purpose.

## Privacy incident handling

A privacy incident should be routed to designated privacy, legal, and incident roles with a clear escalation path. Preserve facts, assess impact, and decide on notification or other required steps under delegated authority.

## Privacy measures

Track impact assessments, inventory coverage, retention compliance, rights-request timeliness, privileged access reviews, supplier privacy reviews, and incident lessons. Reporting must avoid exposing personal data unnecessarily.
