# Cyber risk management and treatment

## Purpose

Risk management helps leaders compare uncertainty and consequences against business objectives. Risk scores are aids for prioritization, not substitutes for judgment.

## Risk statement format

> Because of [cause], [event] could affect [service or asset], resulting in [business consequence].

## Assessment workflow

1. Set context and assumptions
2. Identify scenarios and root causes
3. Assess inherent risk
4. Evaluate current controls
5. Assess residual risk
6. Compare to risk appetite
7. Select treatment: avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept
8. Plan actions and review triggers

## Treatment plan criteria

A treatment plan should include:

- owner and approver
- due date and milestones
- dependency and funding requirements
- interim controls
- success criteria
- review date and escalation triggers

## Acceptance process

Risk acceptance must be time-bound, approved by the appropriate authority, and supported by a documented rationale. It should be revisited when conditions, threats, or service design change.

## Risk register quality

Track material risks, stale assessments, duplicate entries, ownerless issues, overdue actions, and risks outside appetite. Review triggers include incidents, system changes, supplier changes, and new obligations.
