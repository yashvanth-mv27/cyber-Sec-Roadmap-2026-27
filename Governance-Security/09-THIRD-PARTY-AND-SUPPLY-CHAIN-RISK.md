# Third-party and supply-chain risk governance

## Purpose

Supplier risk management is the process of evaluating external service providers and software dependencies before and during use.

## Supplier lifecycle

### 1. Intake and classification
Record service purpose, criticality, data exposure, hosting jurisdictions, and dependencies.

### 2. Due diligence
Review the provider's security and privacy control posture, contractual commitments, incident history, and resilience arrangements.

### 3. Contracting and onboarding
Define responsibilities, data handling, incident notification, audit access, subcontractor controls, and exit provisions.

### 4. Ongoing monitoring
Review assurance status, material changes, incidents, and open findings on a defined schedule.

### 5. Offboarding and exit
Terminate access, recover or delete data under contract requirements, verify transition arrangements, and document closure.

## Fourth-party risk

Assess critical subcontractors and concentration risks in cloud, software, identity, communications, and host-provider dependencies.

## Supplier register

Track supplier ID, owner, criticality, data exposure, contract status, review dates, open findings, and exit readiness.
