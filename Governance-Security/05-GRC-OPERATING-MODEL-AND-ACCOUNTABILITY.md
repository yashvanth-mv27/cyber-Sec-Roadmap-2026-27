# GRC operating model and governance charter

## Purpose

A GRC operating model explains who owns risk, who operates controls, who tests effectiveness, and how portfolio decisions reach leadership.

## Responsibilities

- **Business and technology owners:** own services, risks, and control execution.
- **Security, privacy, legal, and compliance teams:** advise, monitor, challenge, and report.
- **Independent assurance:** provides objective review and challenge.

## Decision forums

Common forums include:

- service control review
- risk review
- privacy/legal incident forum
- security steering committee
- assurance or audit review

## Decision rights

Use a RACI or rights matrix for:

- policy approval
- risk acceptance
- exceptions
- incident response escalation
- external breach notifications
- remediation prioritization
- closing major findings

## Escalation and authority

Define thresholds by impact, risk appetite, data sensitivity, and service criticality. Escalation must include what decision is needed, by whom, by when, and what happens if the decision is delayed.

## Meeting discipline

Record decisions, owners, due dates, and unresolved issues. Keep sensitive data restricted to relevant participants.
